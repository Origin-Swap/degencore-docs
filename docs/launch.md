# Launching a Token on DegenCore

Launching a meme token on DegenCore takes less than a minute. The process is 100% on-chain, permissionless, and transparent.

## Step-by-Step Guide
1. Connect your wallet (MetaMask, Rabby, etc.) to the Ethereum network.
2. Navigate to the **Launch** page.
3. Fill in your token's **Name** and **Ticker**.
4. Upload a **Token Image** and set your **Social Links** (Website, X, Telegram).
5. Choose your **Paired Asset**:
   * **ETH (WETH)**: For native Ethereum pair.
   * **USDC**: For stablecoin pairing.
   * **SUPRA**: For Supra pairing.
6. Set the **Virtual Pair Reserve (VPR)**: This determines the starting price and steepness of your curve. Lower VPR graduates faster.
7. Choose your **Reward Mode**:
   * **Creator Mode**: 1% of all trading fees go directly to you.
   * **Cashback Mode**: 1% of all trading fees are distributed pro-rata to the traders.
8. *(Optional)* Set a **Developer Buy** to acquire initial tokens at the starting price.
9. Click **Launch Token** and sign the transaction.

Your token is now live on the bonding curve. Share the contract address and let the market do its thing!
