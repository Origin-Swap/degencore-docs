# Tokenomics & Supply

Every token launched on DegenCore follows the exact same supply distribution. There are no exceptions, ensuring a fair and transparent environment for all participants.

## Total Supply
**1,000,000,000 tokens** (Fixed supply, no minting function, no owner privileges).

## Supply Allocation
| Allocation | Percentage | Amount | Purpose |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Bonding Curve Sale** | 80% | 800,000,000 | Sold via the bonding curve to the public. |
| **Liquidity Pool** | 20% | 200,000,000 | Reserved for Uniswap LP at graduation. |

*The 20% liquidity allocation is locked into the Uniswap pool and the LP tokens are sent to the `0x...dEaD` address. It can never be sold by the creator or the platform.*

## Trading Fee Distribution (2% Per Trade)
Every buy or sell transaction on the bonding curve incurs a **2% total fee**.
| Recipient | Allocation | Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Platform** | 1.0% | Sent to the DegenCore treasury to fund the ecosystem. |
| **Creator / Cashback** | 1.0% | 100% goes to one destination, chosen by the creator at launch (Creator Mode or Cashback Mode). |

## Anti-Snipe Surtax
* Max 4% extra fee applied **only on buys**.
* Decays linearly over 60 seconds from the moment of launch.
* 100% of this surtax goes directly into the bonding curve's real reserve, increasing the graduation threshold and thickening the liquidity pool for everyone.

## ⚡ Platform Fees Distribution (The 1% Platform Cut)

The 1% platform fee collected from every trade is not hoarded; it is actively recycled back into the DegenCore ecosystem to ensure long-term sustainability and community growth. The platform fee is distributed as follows:

🔹 **30% — Referral Rewards**  
Incentivizes community members and influencers to bring new traders and high-quality meme projects to the platform.

🔹 **10% — Platform Token Burns**  
Used to buy back and burn the native DegenCore platform token (or equivalent ecosystem token). This creates continuous deflationary pressure and rewards long-term holders.

🔹 **10% — Marketing Support for Top Memecoins**  
Allocated to boost the visibility of the most successful and viral tokens launched on DegenCore. This includes funding for influencer campaigns, trend hijacking, and community events for tokens that have graduated.

🔹 **50% — Treasury**  
Retained in the DegenCore DAO Treasury. These funds are used for protocol development, security audits, infrastructure maintenance (RPCs, indexers), and potential future CEX listings.
