# DegenCore: The Fair Meme Launchpad on Ethereum

**DegenCore** is a decentralized, permissionless meme token launchpad built natively on Ethereum. We believe that the era of unfair token distributions—where venture capitalists and insiders buy at rock-bottom prices—must end.

DegenCore introduces a pure **Bonding Curve** mechanism. There are no presales, no team allocations, and no hidden insider deals. Every token launched on DegenCore is subject to the same mathematical rules, ensuring a truly fair playing field for all participants.

## The Problem with Traditional Meme Launches
1. **Sniping & Front-running**: Bots buy tokens instantly at launch, leaving retail investors with heavily inflated prices.
2. **Rug Pulls**: Creators often dump their entire supply or remove liquidity, leaving traders with worthless tokens.
3. **Insider Allocations**: Large portions of the supply are often reserved for teams or VCs, creating massive sell pressure on day one.

## The DegenCore Solution
* **Fair Price Discovery**: The price is governed purely by supply and demand via a constant-product bonding curve.
* **Permanent Liquidity**: Once a token reaches its funding goal (graduation), liquidity is automatically migrated to Uniswap V2 and the LP tokens are **burned forever**.
* **Incentivized Ecosystem**: A transparent 2% trading fee splits rewards between the platform, the creator, and the traders.
